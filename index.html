FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to know any Tibetan already?

No. Tiblingo starts with the alphabet and simple sentences. If you already know some, the jump-ahead test lets you skip forward.

How does Tiblingo teach?

Tiblingo takes inspiration from Duolingo's teaching method: bite-size lessons, learning by doing, instant feedback, and small rewards that keep you coming back, all built around Tibetan.

Is Tiblingo free?

Our aim is a free, fun and effective way to learn Tibetan. We'll share any details on plans closer to launch.

When can I download it?

We're getting ready to publish on the App Store and Google Play. The download buttons below will go live as soon as we do.

Will there be speaking and listening practice?

Listen and Speak practice are on the way and already have a place in the Practice Hub.
